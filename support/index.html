How to play

  1. Tap anywhere on the title screen to start a night.
  2. Drag one finger left and right, anywhere on the screen. The glowing lamp on the rail follows your finger and the star swings on its chain. Lift and put your finger down again whenever you like: the lamp stays where it is.
  3. Sweep wide, then reverse sharply. The chain turns gold and the hit is a CRACK, which breaks glass much faster. Keep cracking within two and a half seconds of each other to build a streak and reach fever: GLOW, BLAZE, NOVA and MORNINGSTAR.
  4. Watch the top of the sky. The silent dark creeps down toward the rail. Breaking glass pushes it back; if it reaches you, you lose a link of your chain.
  5. Count your beads. Splinters, meteors and glass that lands on the lamp also cost a link. Lose the last one and the night ends; tap to start the next one straight away.
  6. Pick a charm when the night pauses and offers three cards. Tap one, then touch the screen to carry on.

Magenta always means danger, and every danger flashes a warning before it lands. After each night the game tells you what hit you and how close you came.

Questions

I can’t hear anything

Morningstar follows your iPhone’s Ring/Silent switch, like most games. If the switch is set to silent, the game stays quiet.

  • Flip the switch back to ring (or, on iPhones with an Action button, turn Silent Mode off), or
  • open Settings (the round button at the top left of the title screen) and turn on Play when silenced.
  • Also check that Sound and Music are on in Settings, and that the speaker button at the top right of a run is not muted.

The game mixes with other audio, so music from another app keeps playing underneath it.

How do I turn haptics (vibration) on or off?

Use the Haptics switch in Settings on the title screen, or in the pause menu during a run. The game taps when you grab, collect gold, reach a fever tier and get hit.

If you feel nothing with Haptics on, check your iPhone’s Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics.

How do I pause?

Tap the pause button at the top left during a run. The game also pauses by itself when you leave the app, take a call, or pull down Notification Center or Control Center. When you resume, a short 3-2-1 countdown gives you time to get your thumb back in place.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded, so progress does not sync between devices by itself, and deleting the app deletes its progress.

To keep it safe or move it to another device, use a save code:

  1. Open Settings → Save data.
  2. Under Export, tap Copy code and keep the code somewhere safe (for example, in Notes). It starts with MS1-.
  3. On the other device, open the same screen, paste the code under Import and tap Merge this save.

Importing checks the code and merges it with the progress already on that device: you keep the best of both, and nothing is overwritten.

How do I start over?

Open Settings → Save data, tap Reset progress, then tap it again within three seconds to confirm. This erases your ranks, items and records on this device; your settings stay. It cannot be undone, so export a save code first if you might want your progress back.

What is “Night #N”?

The Daily challenge: a new night every day at local midnight, with the same layout and modifiers on every iPhone. Practice it as often as you like; when you are ready, start your one scored attempt for the day: its result is the one you can copy and share. Play on consecutive days to grow your streak and unlock streak rewards.

Is the game too flashy or busy for me?

Turn on Reduced motion in Settings. It replaces full-screen flashes and shakes with gentler effects. It starts on automatically if Reduce Motion is on in your iPhone’s Accessibility settings.

Are there ads, in-app purchases or an account?

No, none of them. Everything in the game is unlocked by playing, the app never asks you to sign in, and it collects no data. See the privacy policy.

Does it work offline? Can I play in landscape?

It works completely offline, including in Airplane Mode. The game is designed for one thumb in portrait, so it stays upright when you turn your iPhone.

Report a problem

Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the game version (shown in Settings → About), plus what happened just before the problem. A screenshot helps a lot.
