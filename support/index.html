How to play

  1. Tap anywhere on the title screen to start a night.
  2. Drag one finger left and right, anywhere on the screen. The glowing lamp on the rail follows your finger and the star swings on its chain. Lift and put your finger down again whenever you like: the lamp stays where it is.
  3. Sweep wide, then reverse sharply. The chain turns gold and the hit is a CRACK, which breaks glass much faster. Keep cracking within two and a half seconds of each other to build a streak and reach fever: GLOW, BLAZE, NOVA and MORNINGSTAR.
  4. Watch the top of the sky. The silent dark creeps down toward the rail. Breaking glass pushes it back; if it reaches you, you lose a link of your chain.
  5. Count your beads. Splinters, meteors and glass that lands on the lamp also cost a link. Lose the last one and the night ends; tap to start the next one.
  6. Pick a charm when the night pauses and offers three cards. Tap one, then touch the screen to carry on.

Magenta always means danger, and every danger flashes a warning before it lands. After each night the game tells you what hit you and how close you came.

Questions

I can’t hear anything

First check that Sound and Music are on in the game’s Settings (the round button at the top left of the title screen), and that the speaker button at the top right of a run is not muted.

On iPhone, Morningstar follows your iPhone’s Ring/Silent switch, like most games. If the switch is set to silent, the game stays quiet.

  • Flip the switch back to ring (or, on iPhones with an Action button, turn Silent Mode off), or
  • open Settings in the game and turn on Play when silenced.

On iPhone the game mixes with other audio, so music from another app keeps playing underneath it.

On Android, the game plays at your phone’s media volume, so silent or vibrate mode does not mute it. If it is quiet, turn the media volume up: press the volume up button while the game is on screen, or raise the media slider in the volume panel.

How do I turn haptics (vibration) on or off?

Use the Haptics switch in Settings on the title screen, or in the pause menu during a run. The game taps when you grab, collect gold, reach a fever tier and get hit.

If you feel nothing with Haptics on:

  • On iPhone, check your iPhone’s Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics.
  • On Android, check that vibration is on in your phone’s Settings → Sound & vibration (the name varies by phone), and turn off Battery Saver, which can stop vibration.
How do I pause?

Tap the pause button at the top left during a run. On Android, the Back button pauses a run too. The game also pauses by itself when you leave the app or take a call, and when you pull down Notification Center or Control Center on iPhone, or the notification shade on Android. When you resume, a short 3-2-1 countdown gives you time to get your thumb back in place.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone only. There is no account and the game never uploads your progress, so it does not sync between devices by itself, and deleting the app deletes its progress.

Your phone’s own backup can include it: iCloud Backup on iPhone, or on Android, Auto Backup to your Google account, which can put your progress back when you install the game again or set up a new phone.

To keep it safe or move it to another device, iPhone or Android, use a save code:

  1. Open Settings → Save data.
  2. Under Export, tap Copy code and keep the code somewhere safe (for example, in a notes app). It starts with MS1-.
  3. On the other device, open the same screen, paste the code under Import and tap Merge this save.

Importing checks the code and merges it with the progress already on that device: you keep the best of both, and nothing is overwritten.

How do I start over?

Open Settings → Save data, tap Reset progress, then tap it again within three seconds to confirm. This erases your ranks, items and records on this device; your settings stay. It cannot be undone, so export a save code first if you might want your progress back.

What is “Night #N”?

The Daily challenge: a new night every day at local midnight, with the same layout and modifiers on every phone. Practice it as often as you like; when you are ready, start your one scored attempt for the day: its result is the one you can copy and share. Play on consecutive days to grow your streak and unlock streak rewards.

Is the game too flashy or busy for me?

Turn on Reduced motion in Settings. It replaces full-screen flashes and shakes with gentler effects. On iPhone, it starts on automatically if Reduce Motion is on in your iPhone’s Accessibility settings.

Is there anything to buy? Do I need an account?

No to both. Morningstar is free to play and there are no in-app purchases: shards, the game’s only currency, are earned by playing and cannot be bought, and everything in the game is unlocked by playing. The app never asks you to sign in. The game is supported by ads, which only appear between runs, never while you play.

When do ads appear?

Only between runs, never while you play:

  • After a run, the card can offer WATCH AD with a shard bonus. Watch the short video to the end and the bonus shards are yours; close it early and nothing happens. It is optional, once per run and up to 6 times a day.
  • Now and then, a full-screen ad plays after a finished run, behind a short AD BREAK screen: never in your first 5 runs, at most every 2nd run and at least 3 minutes apart.
  • The title screen shows a small banner at the bottom. It hides as soon as a run starts or a menu opens.

Ads are limited to G-rated content. They need an internet connection, so offline you see none and the game plays the same.

Does the game collect any data?

The game keeps your progress only on your phone and sends nothing about you to me. To show ads, Google’s ad software in the app collects some device data, such as your IP address and your advertising identifier: on iPhone only if you allow tracking, and on Android your phone’s advertising ID, unless you have deleted it. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I change tracking, my advertising ID or my privacy choices?

Tracking on iPhone: open your iPhone’s Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and switch Morningstar on or off.

Advertising ID on Android: Android has no tracking prompt. To reset or delete your phone’s advertising ID, open Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the phone. Once it is deleted, apps can no longer use it.

Ad consent (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) and the US “Do Not Sell or Share” opt-out: open Settings → About in the game and tap Privacy choices. It appears only where the law in your region requires it.

The game plays the same whatever you choose.

Does it work offline? Can I play in landscape?

It works completely offline, including in Airplane Mode. Ads, including the optional videos, need a connection, so offline they simply don’t appear. The game is designed for one thumb in portrait, so it is always played with the phone upright.

Report an ad

Saw an ad in Morningstar that seems inappropriate, offensive or not right for a general audience? Please tell me. Ads in the app are limited to G-rated content, and your report helps me block anything that slips through. In the app, Settings → About → Report an ad opens this section.

Email me and include:

  • the date and roughly what time you saw the ad;
  • what the ad showed or advertised (the brand or app name helps most), and where it appeared: the banner on the title screen, the full-screen ad break, or the optional video;
  • a screenshot, if you can take one.
