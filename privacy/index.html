Privacy Policy

Morningstar: Night of Glass · Effective October 5, 2026

The short version: the Morningstar app does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

Morningstar: Night of Glass (“Morningstar”, “the app”) is an independent game made by Edward Carreno (“I”, “me”). This policy covers the Morningstar app for iPhone and this website.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, sell, share or transmit any personal information or usage data. In particular:

  • No network access. The app makes no network requests. The game, its graphics and its music are all inside the app, and it plays fully offline, including in Airplane Mode. The only links in the app, Privacy Policy and Support under Settings → About, open this website in Safari, outside the app.
  • No accounts. You never sign in, and the app never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. There are no third-party SDKs, no advertising identifier, no cookies and no tracking of any kind, inside the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No device permissions. The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts. It plays sound and uses the iPhone’s haptic engine for vibration feedback, both locally.

What stays on your iPhone

To remember your progress, the app saves a small game file in its own storage on your device: your best scores and times, ranks, missions, unlocked items, daily streak and settings (such as sound, music and haptics). This file never leaves your iPhone through the app, and I cannot see it. Your iPhone’s own backups (such as iCloud Backup, if you use it) may include it, under Apple’s control.

  • Erase your progress: in the game, open Settings → Save data → Reset progress, then tap again to confirm. Your settings are kept.
  • Erase everything: delete the app from your iPhone. Its saved file is deleted with it.
  • Save codes: Settings → Save data can show your progress as a text code (starting with MS1-) so you can move it to another device. The code is created on your iPhone and goes only where you paste it.
  • Copying results: if you copy a Daily result or a save code, the text is placed on your device’s clipboard at your request. The app does not send it anywhere.

Because I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to delete, correct or export on request. If you have a question about that, write to me and I will answer it.

Apple and the App Store

You download Morningstar from Apple’s App Store, which is run by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. Apple may give developers aggregated, anonymous App Store statistics (for example, the number of downloads) and, only if you have chosen to share them in your iPhone’s settings, crash reports. These do not identify you to me, and I use them only to understand how the app is doing and to fix bugs.

Children

Morningstar is made for everyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). There are no ads, purchases, chat or social features, and the only links out of the app are this privacy policy and the support page.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics, trackers, fonts or scripts from other services. Like any web host, GitHub may keep server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. If you email me, I use your message and address only to reply to you.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and its effective date before that version is released. Any data collection would have to be described here and in the App Store’s privacy details first.
