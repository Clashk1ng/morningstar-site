Morningstar

Night of Glass

Swing a star on a chain through a night made of stained glass. Crack the panes, push back the silent dark, and see the dawn.

A one-thumb arcade game for iPhone

  • Free to play
  • Nothing to buy
  • No account
  • Plays offline

Swipe for more

One thumb. One star. One night.

  1. Drag anywhere on the screen, with either hand. The glowing lamp on the rail follows your thumb and the star swings on its chain.
  2. Sweep wide, then reverse. The chain flares gold and the hit is a CRACK: glass bursts, your streak climbs and the music swells.
  3. Make noise to hold back the dark. Every pane you break pushes the silent dark back up the sky. Hold out from 11 PM to 6 AM and see the dawn.

A night that never plays the same

Glass with a mind of its own

Iron that only a CRACK can dent, splitters, prisms that fire beams, phantoms, mirrors and lanterns full of gold.

Bells and meteors

Giant stained-glass bells to break before they land, meteors with fair warning, and six skies from Dusk Glass to Daybreak.

Build your star

Draft charms as the night deepens: a burning comet tail, lightning between panes, a second star. Pair the right two for a hidden evolution.

Moons and dailies

Moons that change what your thumb does, a fresh numbered Night challenge every day, missions, ranks and a constellation to complete.

Made with care

The whole game lives on your iPhone and plays offline, with music composed live as you play and haptics for every grab, fever and hit. There is nothing to buy and no account: every star, chain and sky is earned by playing.

Morningstar is supported by ads from Google AdMob, and they only appear between runs, never while you play: an optional video after a run for bonus shards, now and then a short ad break, and a small banner on the title screen.
